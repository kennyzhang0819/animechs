Animechs · shot art

Four bullets that are not Mindustry's

The stock bullet pair is a symmetric lozenge lifted from Mindustry's sprites. These four replace it under the same contract, so nothing downstream changes: a white front and a longer white back, drawn nose-up into one 52×52 cell, tinted with the ammo's two colours, stretched onto the shot's across × along box, shrunk on its life, turned to its heading.

Everything below is rendered by the same code that would pack the atlas. Each option is one drawing function beside canisterBullet in game/atlas.ts; the recolours, stretches and life strips are the renderer's own arithmetic applied to it, at the zoom a run starts on. Nothing here is a mock-up of what it might look like.

The contract every option is drawn to

Two white layers, two tints

An ammo is a pair of colours over a pair of shapes. The renderer pushes back in backColor then front in frontColor on one quad. Where the back sticks out past the front is the only shading a shot ever gets, so the design lives in that rim.

Stretch is the quad, not the art

The whole 52px cell is mapped onto across × along, so a 7×9 pellet and a 16×23 slug are the same pixels at different aspect ratios. Straight edges survive that; circles become ellipses. Then shrinkY eats half the length over the shot's life.

Alpha is free

The tint multiplies colour and alpha, and the atlas is antialiased. A soft tail or a translucent halo costs nothing and still recolours. The stock pair never used this.

How it ships

Shipped: the plain bolt (02b) is boltBullet in game/atlas.ts next to canisterBullet, and the two lines that pack UV_BULLET and UV_BULLET_BACK draw it instead of loading the PNGs, which are gone. The same capsule then took over shell and missile at 36 (the missile with fins on its back), the emp round became a drawn disc, and the boss missile is drawn in the Sovereign's materials, so no shot on the field is a Mindustry sprite. BULLET_REGIONS, every ammo's sprite block, the LOD floors and the swarm's ShotLook all keep working, because the cell is still 52 white pixels facing the same way. The two PNGs come off the manifest. The same treatment then fits shell and missile, which are the same lozenge at 36.
